About This Visualization

The Decision-Making Visualizer uses quantum probability wave functions to demonstrate how our choices create branching paths of possibilities. Each decision exists as a superposition of potential outcomes until we "observe" it by making a choice, similar to how quantum particles exist in multiple states until measured.

This visualization uses φ-harmonic principles to demonstrate:

  • Quantum Superposition: Multiple potential outcomes exist simultaneously
  • Wave Function Collapse: How decisions collapse possibilities into actual outcomes
  • Coherence Level: The clarity and stability of your decision-making field
  • φ-Harmonic Resonance: How different frequencies affect your decision patterns

Each frequency creates a unique decision field:

  • 432Hz (Ground): Practical, survival-oriented decisions
  • 528Hz (Creation): Creative, innovative choices
  • 594Hz (Heart): Relationship and emotional decisions
  • 672Hz (Voice): Communication and self-expression choices
  • 720Hz (Vision): Insightful, far-sighted decisions
  • 768Hz (Unity): Harmonious, integrative choices

How To Use

Enter Your Decision: Type the decision you're considering in the input field.

Add Options: Enter different possible choices for your decision.

Adjust Coherence: Slide the coherence level to see how clarity affects your outcome probabilities. Higher coherence creates more defined outcome paths.

Select Frequency: Change the frequency to see how different consciousness states might influence your decision field.

Explore Outcomes: Click on decision nodes to follow specific paths and see potential future outcomes.

Reset: Click the reset button to start over with a new decision.
