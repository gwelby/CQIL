Knowledge Transmission

About This Visualization

Knowledge doesn't simply progress linearly - it flows through civilizations in patterns that follow the golden ratio (φ). This visualization maps historical knowledge traditions at φ-harmonic intervals, showing how certain fundamental insights have emerged independently across different cultures.

By using φ-harmonic frequencies, we can categorize different types of knowledge:

  • 432Hz (Ground): Foundation knowledge - agriculture, construction, basic survival
  • 528Hz (Creation): Creative knowledge - art, music, invention, innovation
  • 594Hz (Heart): Ethical knowledge - philosophy, ethics, social organization
  • 672Hz (Voice): Communication knowledge - language, writing, information transmission
  • 720Hz (Vision): Insight knowledge - mathematics, science, systematic understanding
  • 768Hz (Unity): Unity knowledge - spiritual traditions, mystical understanding

Notice how similar patterns of knowledge emerge at different times in history, often following φ-harmonic relationships in their timing and development.

How To Use

Explore Time Periods: Use the time slider to travel through history, from ancient civilizations to the present day.

Select Knowledge Types: Click on the different knowledge type buttons to filter the visualization by knowledge domain.

Discover Connections: Hover over knowledge nodes to see how they connect to other traditions across time and space.

Observe Patterns: Notice how the spiraling pattern of knowledge transmission follows φ-harmonic principles, with similar insights emerging at mathematically related intervals.
