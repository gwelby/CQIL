Welcome to the Visualization Gallery

Hi, I'm Greg. I've created this collection of interactive visualizations to help you understand the fascinating connections between how we think, how we communicate, and how we perceive reality. These visualizations use principles from quantum physics to make abstract concepts easier to understand.

Each visualization explores a different aspect of human experience - from how we communicate with others to how our thought patterns influence our perception of the world. I've designed these tools to help make complex ideas more accessible through direct visual experience.
